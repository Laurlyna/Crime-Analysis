# Crime Analysis 

## Project Overview
Analysis of Los Angeles crime data (2020-2025) using SQL and Excel to uncover crime trends, high risk areas, victim patterns and case resolution insights.

## Objective
To analyze crime patterns in Los Angeles, identify high risk areas and time periods, understand victim demographics, and build an interactive dashboard for public safety insights.

## Dataset Information
- Source: Los Angeles Crime Data (2020-2025)
- Size: 1,000,000+ rows
- Fields: 28 columns including crime type, location, victim details, weapon used and case status

## Tools Used
- MySQL — Data cleaning & Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- Excel — Interactive dashboard & data visualization

## Business Questions
- Which year/season had the highest crime rate?
- Which areas had the highest crime frequency?
- Which age group and gender were most affected?
- Which crimes were most common?
- What weapons were most used?
- When were crimes most likely to occur?
- What was the case closure rate?

## Data Cleaning Process
- Corrected data types
- Handled missing values
- Standardized fields
- Renamed columns
- Cleaned time and date formats
- Grouped victim age categories

## EDA Process
- Analyzed crime trends by year and season
- Identified top 5 high risk areas
- Examined victim demographics by gender and age
- Investigated most common crime types
- Analyzed weapon usage patterns
- Examined time of day crime patterns
- Reviewed case status and closure rates

## Key Insights
- Central and 77th Street are the highest risk areas
- Vehicle theft and battery assault are the most common crimes
- Adults are the most affected age group
- Street locations account for the highest number of incidents
- The majority of cases are still under active investigation

## Recommendations
- Increase patrol presence in Central and 77th Street areas
- Focus prevention efforts on vehicle theft
- Improve case closure rates through better resource allocation

## Limitations
Some fields contained missing or coded values, which were handled carefully to preserve data integrity during analysis.
